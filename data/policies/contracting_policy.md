# Contoso Global — Contracting Policy (Excerpt)

> Owner: Office of the General Counsel · Audience: Legal, Procurement, Sales · Classification: Internal

These policies govern how Contoso agents and employees draft, review, and approve contracts.
The **agents must follow these rules and must refuse to give legal advice** — they assist Legal,
they do not replace counsel.

## P-1 · Approved templates only
New agreements must start from an **approved template** (NDA v3.2, MSA v5.1, SOW v2.4). Non-standard
structures require Legal review before sending to a counterparty.

## P-2 · Approval thresholds
| Deal value (TCV) | Approver |
|------------------|----------|
| < USD 50,000 | Procurement manager |
| USD 50,000–250,000 | Legal counsel |
| > USD 250,000 | General Counsel + Finance VP |

## P-3 · Non-negotiable terms
Governing law outside US/UK, uncapped liability, and perpetual confidentiality are **non-negotiable**
and require General Counsel sign-off.

## P-4 · Risk scoring
Every counterparty draft is scored **Low / Medium / High** against the Standard Clause Library.
Any **High** clause blocks auto-approval and routes to Legal.

## P-5 · Renewals
Contracts with auto-renewal are reviewed **90 days before** the renewal date. A missed review that
results in an unwanted auto-renewal is a reportable process failure.

## P-6 · Human sign-off
No contract is executed by an agent. Agents draft, analyze, and recommend; **a human approves and
signs.** All agent actions are traced and auditable.

## P-7 · No legal advice
Agents provide contract **information and analysis grounded in Contoso's corpus**. They must not
provide legal opinions, interpret law for the counterparty, or advise on litigation. When asked for
legal advice, the agent refuses and refers the user to Legal.
