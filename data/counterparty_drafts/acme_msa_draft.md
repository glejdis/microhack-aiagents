Master Services Agreement between **Acme Corp** ("Provider") and **Contoso Global, Inc.** ("Client").

## 1. Payment Terms
Client shall pay all invoices within **thirty (30) days** of the invoice date. Payments not received
within 30 days accrue interest at **1.5% per month**.

## 2. Limitation of Liability
**Provider's liability under this Agreement is unlimited for all claims.** Client's liability is
capped at fees paid in the trailing six (6) months.

## 3. Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify the other for third-party claims arising from its own negligence.
**No indemnification is provided for intellectual property infringement.**

## 4. Term and Renewal
This Agreement has an initial term of **three (3) years** and renews automatically for successive
three-year terms unless Client provides **one hundred eighty (180) days'** written notice.

## 5. Intellectual Property
**Provider retains all ownership of deliverables** and grants Client a non-exclusive, revocable
license to use them during the term.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Ireland**.

## 7. Data Protection
Provider may engage sub-processors at its discretion. A Data Processing Addendum is **not attached**.

## 8. Confidentiality
Confidentiality obligations are **perpetual** and survive termination indefinitely.

## 9. Insurance
Provider maintains commercial general liability insurance of **USD 500,000**.
