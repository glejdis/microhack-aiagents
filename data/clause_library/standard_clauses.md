# Contoso Global — Standard Clause Library

Enterprise-standard positions used to benchmark counterparty drafts. The **Clause & Risk agent**
compares incoming clauses to these standards and flags deviations with a risk score.

---

## CL-01 · Payment Terms
**Standard:** Net 60 from receipt of undisputed invoice.
**Acceptable range:** Net 45–Net 75.
**Red flag:** Net 30 or shorter (cash-flow impact); payment on signature.

## CL-02 · Limitation of Liability
**Standard:** Cap equal to fees paid in the trailing 12 months; carve-outs for confidentiality,
IP infringement, and indemnification.
**Red flag:** Uncapped liability; caps below 12 months' fees; no carve-outs.

## CL-03 · Indemnification
**Standard:** Supplier indemnifies Contoso for third-party claims from negligence, willful
misconduct, and IP infringement.
**Red flag:** Mutual or Contoso-only indemnity; no IP infringement coverage.

## CL-04 · Term & Auto-Renewal
**Standard:** 1-year initial term; auto-renews for 1-year terms; 90 days' non-renewal notice.
**Red flag:** Auto-renewal notice period > 90 days; multi-year lock-in without termination for
convenience.

## CL-05 · Governing Law
**Standard:** State of Washington, USA.
**Acceptable:** Delaware, New York (US); England & Wales (EMEA deals).
**Red flag:** Non-US/UK jurisdiction without Legal approval.

## CL-06 · Intellectual Property
**Standard:** Deliverables are works made for hire; ownership vests in Contoso on payment.
**Red flag:** Supplier retains ownership; license-back only.

## CL-07 · Data Protection
**Standard:** Processing per Contoso instructions + DPA; GDPR compliance where applicable.
**Red flag:** No DPA; sub-processing without notice; data stored outside approved regions.

## CL-08 · Confidentiality Term
**Standard:** Obligations survive 3 years after disclosure.
**Red flag:** Perpetual confidentiality; survival < 2 years.

## CL-09 · Termination
**Standard:** Termination for material breach with 30-day cure; termination for convenience with
60 days' notice.
**Red flag:** No termination for convenience; cure period > 60 days.

## CL-10 · Insurance
**Standard:** Commercial general liability ≥ USD 2,000,000.
**Red flag:** Coverage below USD 1,000,000; no cyber coverage for data processors.
